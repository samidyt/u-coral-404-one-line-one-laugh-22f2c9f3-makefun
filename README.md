One funny line, 200 characters or fewer, that lands entirely on its own — my entry for make.fun's "One Line, One Laugh" bounty.

> My smart fridge keeps suggesting recipes based on what is inside it, which is just ice and a single, very confident pickle.

A smart fridge earnestly suggesting recipes sounds helpful — until the inventory turns out to be just ice and one pickle, and the pickle is the confident one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` validates the line against the gate (exactly one line, 1–200 characters, non-blank), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it.
